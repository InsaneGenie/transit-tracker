NY Transit Live privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026.

NY Transit Live shows where New York's buses and trains are right now. It works without an account and does not collect, sell or share personal information.

What the app does not collect

There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts or anything else about you.

Your location

If you tap the location button and allow it, the app uses your location to show where you are on the map. Your location stays on your phone: it is never sent to our server or anyone else.

What our server sees

To show vehicles, the app asks our server for the buses and trains inside the part of the map on your screen. That request contains the map area, not your location. Like any website, our hosting provider (Render) may keep short-lived logs of requests, including IP addresses, to run and protect the service. We don't use them to identify anyone.

Settings saved on your phone

The lines you choose to show are saved on your phone so they are still chosen next time. They never leave it.

Where the data comes from

Vehicle positions and arrival times come from the public data feeds of the Metropolitan Transportation Authority (MTA). NY Transit Live is not made by or affiliated with the MTA.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
